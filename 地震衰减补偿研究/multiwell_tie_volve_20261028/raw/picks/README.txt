The directory /project/volvepub/Geophysical_Interpretations/Wells
contains the following two files:

1) Well_picks_Volve_v1.dat: 
Contains most important well picks for Volve wells.
Some well picks occur more than 1 time in a single well (indicated with Obs#).
This is due to the well crossing in and out of a formation several times (e.g.
due to faults, or horizontal well path).

2) Well_perforations_Volve.dat:
Contains well perforation intervals.
