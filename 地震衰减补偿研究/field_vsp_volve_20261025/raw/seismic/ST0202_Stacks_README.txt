The directory /project/volvepub/Seismic/ST0202/Stacks
contains raw and final stacks from the 2008 procesing of ST0202. This was KPSDM
of PZ and PS data. Stacks are in PP time and depth.

For the final PS data, only full stacks are available, for the PZ data full, near,
near-mid, mid, and far stacks are available.
